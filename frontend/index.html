About LabelGuard AI

Ensuring Fair Packaging Standards & Consumer Protection

LabelGuard AI is designed to assist consumers and enforcement authorities in verifying mandatory packaging disclosures. Powered by optical character recognition (OCR) and computer-vision heuristics, the system checks critical declarations against the statutory provisions of the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

⚖️

Legal Metrology Compliance

Verifies that pre-packaged consumer goods bear mandatory declarations including price, net quantity, packaging dates, and country of origin without unlawful tampering.

🔍

Automated OCR & Layout Detection

Uses lightweight object detection and OCR to parse printed text from physical packaging labels with strict confidence thresholding to prevent false certifications.

🛡️

Role-Based Transparency

Equips citizens with consumer grievance support and provides authorized government inspectors with formal inspection notices carrying legal evidentiary standing.

Verification Workflow

How LabelGuard AI Operates

A four-step general packaging compliance workflow engineered for transparent verification.

1

Image Capture

Upload a photo of the product package or use the live camera scanner to capture the label surface.

2

Region & Text OCR

The pipeline locates candidate declaration regions and extracts printed textual content with confidence scoring.

3

Legal Rule Checks

Evaluates parsed fields against mandatory requirements: tax-inclusive MRP, standard net quantity, date, and country of origin.

4

Audit Report

Review the annotated inspection visual, check results breakdown, and download the official compliance PDF.

Platform Capabilities

Verified Compliance Features

Tested capabilities implemented directly in LabelGuard AI.

🏷️

MRP & Inclusive Taxes

Detects numerical pricing, rupee/currency indicators, and validates the presence of mandatory "inclusive of all taxes" declaration.

⚖️

Standard Metric Net Quantity

Validates net weight and volume declarations against standard metric units (g, kg, ml, l, or count) as prescribed by packaging regulations.

📅

Date of Manufacture Format

Checks validity of manufacturing or packaging dates in standard formats (MM/YYYY) for consumer freshness safety.

🌐

Country of Origin Verification

Identifies country of origin declarations, highlighting imported commodities requiring clear source jurisdiction labeling.

📑

Official Audit PDF Generation

Automatically generates comprehensive PDF compliance audit reports formatted for evidentiary archives and dispute documentation.

🛡️

Role-Enforced Workspaces

Strict backend permission verification distinguishing between consumer product safety inquiries and regulatory officer audits.

Privacy Policy

LabelGuard AI processes uploaded packaging imagery solely to perform automated optical character recognition and legal metrology verification.

Uploaded physical packaging scans are processed transiently and removed from temporary working buffers. We do not collect private personal data from packaging labels.

Audit records generated by authorized Government Inspectors carry evidentiary standing under Section 65B of the Indian Evidence Act and are preserved strictly in accordance with statutory requirements.

Terms of Use

LabelGuard AI provides computational packaging compliance assistance under the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

While computer vision heuristics and optical character recognition are calibrated for high precision, determinations marked as REVIEW indicate that physical visual verification by a qualified inspector is recommended.
